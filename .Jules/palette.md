# Palette's Journal - UX & Accessibility Learnings

## 2025-05-14 - UX 'Delight' Pattern
**Learning:** Introducing an artificial delay (e.g., 800ms) for AI generation simulations, paired with a clear "Generating..." state, significantly improves user perception of the feature's complexity and value.
**Action:** Always pair simulated async processes with loading states and disabled button interactions to prevent multiple submissions and provide clear feedback.
