## 2025-06-10 - [Accessibility and Interaction Feedback in AdCreator]
**Learning:** Form fields without IDs and associated labels are inaccessible to screen readers. Additionally, instantaneous "AI" generation can feel jarring; a small artificial delay with clear loading states improves perceived value and delight.
**Action:** Always use `useId` for form accessibility and implement loading states with spinners for simulated or real async operations.
