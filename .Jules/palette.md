## 2026-07-09 - [UX Delight: Simulated AI Delay]
**Learning:** Introducing a small artificial delay (e.g., 800ms) with a loading state for AI-driven operations significantly improves 'perceived value'. Users often associate instant results with lower complexity or quality.
**Action:** Always pair 'Magic' or 'AI' buttons with a disabled loading state (spinner + 'Generating...') and a minimum artificial delay to manage user expectations and provide feedback.
