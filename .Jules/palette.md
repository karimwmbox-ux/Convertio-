## 2025-05-14 - Accessibility for Form Interactivity
**Learning:** Applying the `cursor-pointer` class to form `<label>` elements when they are linked to inputs via `htmlFor` provides essential visual feedback for interactivity, especially in dark-themed professional dashboards.
**Action:** Consistently use `useId` for accessible label-input associations and ensure labels are visually interactive.
