## 2025-05-14 - [Ad Creative Character Limits]
**Learning:** Meta Ads have specific recommended character limits (125 for body, 40 for headline). Providing immediate visual feedback with color-coded counters (gray -> orange -> red) helps users optimize their creative for the platform without leaving the app.
**Action:** Always research platform-specific constraints when building specialized creation tools and surface them as non-intrusive micro-UX guides.
