## 2025-05-14 - Ad Creation UX Enhancements
**Learning:** Meta Ads have strict character recommendations (40 for headline, 125 for primary text). Providing real-time visual feedback (counters and color changes) significantly improves the user's ability to create valid ads without leaving the interface. Pairing AI simulations with a small artificial delay and loading state increases the perceived value and complexity of the 'magic' feature.
**Action:** Always include character constraints and loading states for domain-specific inputs and AI-powered operations.
