## 2025-05-15 - [Enhancing AI Feedback with 'Delight' Delays]
**Learning:** Instantaneous AI results can feel 'too easy' for users, whereas a small artificial delay (800-1000ms) with a loading state improves the perceived value and complexity of the feature.
**Action:** Always implement a brief loading state for AI generation features, even if the client-side logic is fast.

## 2025-05-15 - [Form Accessibility in Dark UI]
**Learning:** In dark-themed dashboards, interactive elements like labels for inputs are often overlooked for clickability.
**Action:** Use `htmlFor` for all labels and apply `cursor-pointer` to both labels and inputs to provide clear visual feedback in dense UIs.
