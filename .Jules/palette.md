## 2025-05-15 - [Enhanced AdCreator UX & A11y]
**Learning:** Adding artificial delays (e.g. 800ms) to AI generation features significantly improves perceived complexity and value, while character counts are essential for ad managers working with platform limits.
**Action:** Always pair simulated async states with `RefreshCw` (spinner) and ensure all form labels use `htmlFor` with `useId` for screen reader compatibility.
